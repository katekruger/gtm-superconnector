# Account criteria

- Primary ICP: 2-15-person software teams; up to 30 only when the evidence supports a focused team or pod.
- Require evidence of Claude Code or Codex use.
- Prefer open-source, AI-tooling, and developer-workflow evidence.
- Contact priority: CTO or technical cofounder, founder, engineering leader, developer-productivity leader, then founding/staff engineer.
- Never treat an inferred title, email, or company attribute as verified.
